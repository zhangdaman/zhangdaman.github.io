The Bridge Between Craft and Computation

The role of the product manager is shifting from gathering requirements to navigating probabilities. In an era where technical feasibility dictates strategy, understanding the underlying architecture of AI is no longer optional—it is the foundation of the craft. My practice is built on the belief that product management in artificial intelligence requires the intellectual rigor of an engineer combined with the narrative clarity of an editor.

Shipping is not merely a milestone; it is the fundamental mechanism through which we learn. In non-deterministic systems, hypotheses cannot be validated on a whiteboard. They must be subjected to the friction of reality. By treating shipping as an iterative discipline rather than a discrete event, we can safely navigate the ambiguities inherent in large language models and generative applications.

Finally, I believe in the power of learning in public. The frontier of AI is advancing too rapidly for siloed knowledge. Documenting failures, outlining architectural trade-offs, and publishing product frameworks openly accelerates collective understanding. The builder-scholar ethos demands that we not only construct the future but also leave a legible blueprint for those who follow.

Career Arc

My trajectory into AI product management wasn't a calculated pivot, but a necessary evolution. I began my career building traditional deterministic SaaS platforms, where success was defined by rigidly structured data models and predictable user flows. The turning point arrived when I was tasked with integrating early semantic search capabilities into a mature enterprise product. The sheer unruliness of the system—the shift from discrete logic to probabilistic reasoning—was initially terrifying, then deeply compelling. I realized that the future of product design would not be about anticipating every user action, but about constructing intelligent orchestration layers that could adapt dynamically. This realization catalyzed a deliberate immersion into machine learning fundamentals, transitioning my focus entirely toward applied AI, agentic workflows, and the tooling required to evaluate them.

Technical Focus

Current technical investigations and active engineering focus areas:

  • RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) Optimizing chunking strategies, embedding models, and hybrid search pipelines to reduce hallucination and ground outputs in enterprise corpora.
  • Agent Orchestration Designing multi-agent frameworks using LangChain and LlamaIndex. Focusing on deterministic routing within probabilistic bounds.
  • Evals & Telemetry Building rigorous evaluation harnesses. Moving beyond vibes-based assessment to quantitative metrics for model performance and safety.
  • Ads & Growth Surfaces Exploring native integration of generative UI within performance marketing flows to drive contextual conversion.
