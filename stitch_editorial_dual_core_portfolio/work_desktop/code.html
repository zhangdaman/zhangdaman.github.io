§ 02 — SHIPPED

AdPilot

Q3 2023 // GTM STRATEGY

An autonomous campaign management tool that reduced CPA by 22% for mid-market e-commerce brands by dynamically reallocating budget based on predictive LTV models.

PROBLEM

Mid-market merchants lacked the data science resources to optimize multi-channel spend in real-time, leading to high customer acquisition costs on saturated networks.

WHAT I DID

Led the 0-1 product development. Authored the PRD, defined the scoring heuristics with data science, and managed the beta program with 15 pilot customers.

DECISIONS

Opted for a 'human-in-the-loop' approval UI initially rather than full autonomy to build trust. This delayed launch by 3 weeks but resulted in 90% retention post-beta.

OUTCOME

  • $2.4M ARR generated within first 6 months.
  • Adopted by 40% of the active user base.

§ 03 — KILLED

Project X

SUNSET: Q1 2022

An ambitious attempt to build a social network layer on top of a B2B SaaS platform to encourage cross-company procurement collaboration.

PROBLEM

Procurement teams were isolated, often duplicating vendor vetting processes across different organizations within the same industry.

WHAT I DID

Designed a 'shared vendor reputation' system allowing users to anonymously rate and review suppliers, visible to an opt-in network of peers.

WHY I KILLED IT

Legal and compliance blockers. Enterprise customers fundamentally refused to share supplier data, even anonymized, citing competitive advantage and strict NDA violations. The core hypothesis was flawed for the target market.

WHAT IT TAUGHT ME

Validation must go beyond user desire and encompass institutional constraints. Users loved the idea in concept, but General Counsel killed it in practice. Always involve legal early in B2B network effects features.
